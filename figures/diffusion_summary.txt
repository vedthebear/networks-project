METHODOLOGY
----------------------------------------
  Reddit:   directed edge A -> B when a post in subreddit A
            contains a hyperlink to subreddit B.
  Moltbook: directed edge A -> B when submolt A is the first
            community to adopt a hashtag later seen in submolt B.
  Edge weight = number of hashtags where A preceded B.
